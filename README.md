# My Favorite Movie: Hidden Figures

## About the Movie

My favorite movie is **Hidden Figures**. It is a historical drama movie released in *2016*. It tells the story of three African-American women who worked at NASA.

## Why I Like It

I like this movie because it shows the importance of hard work, education, and teamwork. It also teaches us to believe in ourselves and never give up.

## My Favorite Characters

- **Katherine Johnson** - She is good at mathematics.
- **Dorothy Vaughan** - She learns computer programming.
- **Mary Jackson** - She works hard to become an engineer.

## Lessons I Learned

1. Believe in yourself.
2. Never give up on your dreams.
3. Respect other people.
4. Keep learning new things.

## My Favorite Lesson

> Hard work and determination can help us achieve our goals.
